## Notes for Suno generation

- **Section 1**: The challenge is to make the music sound bright while the lyrics hint at sadness. Reinforce in metatag: `[major key string swell sounds happy but vocals carry quiet sadness, last appearance of love theme]`
- **Section 2**: Spoken word, NOT sung. Critical to repeat: `[spoken not sung, conversational, dry close-mic, like sitting on a couch having a real talk]`
- **Section 3**: Pure instrumental. Suno may try to add humming. Repeat: `[string quartet only, no vocals, no humming, no piano, mechanical repeats]`
- **Section 4**: Spoken monologues alternating, with sample sounds between. The cat bell at the end is the emotional core. Suno may struggle with sample sounds; if so, accept and add samples in DAW post.
- **Trailing piano**: The motif D-A-B-G played slow-stretched is critical. Mention it explicitly in metatag.
