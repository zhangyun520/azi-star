Anti-suite four-section segment about ending a marriage with grace, instruments leaving one by one. Four-note motif D-A-B-G appears as retrograde of an earlier piece. Section 1: full chamber ensemble with male tenor and female soprano singing as if pretending nothing is wrong, D-flat major 4/4 84 BPM, lush string swell for the last time. Section 2: piano and spoken word recitative in Mandarin no singing, soft cello plucks distant flute, rubato intimate close-mic, two voices alternating honest hushed conversation, motif appears first time on solo piano. Section 3: string quartet only no vocals no piano no humming, mechanical repeats by first violin, cold contractual, F minor 4/4 90 BPM. Section 4: piano with everyday samples cup on table umbrella keys cat bell, male and female alternating monologues spoken not sung, slow 4/4 66 BPM, motif stretched four times longer. Multilingual Mandarin Cantonese Japanese Korean. Mood: graceful unwinding, tenderness in letting go, never melodramatic.
